14 / 14  ·  The ask

I understand the vendor lock-in, and am not naive about the political nature of the situation we have here.

I wanted to use the opportunity of the SheepAI platform — supported by Grad Split itself — to shine a light on the issue, humbly offer our help, and maybe even get some face-time with the stakeholders to try solve the issue.

Because the people working at City Hall, and the citizens of the most beautiful city in the world, deserve better. Especially in 2026.
